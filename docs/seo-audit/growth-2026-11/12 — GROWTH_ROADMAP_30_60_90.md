- **SEO analyst:** save date-matched 28d/3m GSC query × page × device/country exports. Reconcile query coverage and current property totals. Leading KPI: dated, repeatable baseline; no target CTR before segmentation.

## Days 61–90 — Expand only where evidence supports (to 06/02/2027)

- Enrich selected code pages with strong exact-code demand, verified image and useful product information; preserve healthy canonical/code equity.
- Assess Local SEO after GBP/address verification and route-level post-release crawling.

- If tracking/CRM joins are reliable, connect qualified inquiries and orders with organic landing cohorts; otherwise report clicks/leads only.
- Refresh top-20 rankings based on current evidence and owner dependencies.

## Outcome KPIs

1. **Visibility:** relevant non-brand impressions, useful URL cohort crawl/index status, position distribution.
2. **Acquisition:** organic clicks, qualified sessions and image/AI referrals separately.
3. **Conversion:** valid quote completions, click-to-submit ratio, qualified inquiries.
4. **Revenue:** orders/amount only after actual attribution exists.

No revenue or lead-volume forecast is possible without business conversion data.
