# GEO off-site strategy

This document separates actions that can be shipped from this repository from actions that require a verified business owner or a third-party platform. It is an evidence plan, not a backlink quota.

## Repository-complete foundation

- Keep the canonical domain, business name, phone, address and service-area wording identical across the website, `knowledge.json`, JSON-LD and downloadable references.
- Publish only first-party evidence: verified material specifications, authentic project photos, CNC file examples and dated catalogue updates.
- Maintain `/sitemap.xml`, `/robots.txt`, `/llms.txt` and `/knowledge.json`; run the SEO audit after every content release.
- Use IndexNow only for changed URLs and keep the key outside source control.

## Owner or platform actions

1. **Google Business Profile**: verify the real address/service area, primary category, phone, hours and website; add only authentic photos and services.
2. **Bing Places and Bing Webmaster Tools**: verify the same NAP data, submit the sitemap, enable IndexNow if the account supports it, and review crawl/indexation reports.
3. **Google Search Console**: verify the domain property, submit the sitemap, inspect the homepage, product pages and CNC pages, and export query/page data for the content backlog.
4. **Supplier corroboration**: ask real suppliers or manufacturers to list Tùng Phát only where a current relationship can be documented. Do not claim dealership status without written proof.
5. **Local citations**: maintain consistent NAP on reputable Vietnamese business directories and map providers; remove or correct stale duplicates.
6. **Social/entity profiles**: link official Facebook/Zalo/YouTube profiles from the website and use the same display name and contact details.
7. **Reviews**: request reviews from real customers after completed work. Preserve platform URLs and never gate, script or fabricate reviews.
8. **Original research and PR**: pitch verified material comparison data, a consented CNC case study or a practical file-preparation guide to relevant trade publications.

## Evidence and governance

- Keep invoices, supplier confirmations, consent for project images and review URLs in the business document system.
- Add a source and `dateModified` whenever a catalogue or specification changes.
- Prefer one useful, attributable reference over many low-quality directory links.
- Re-audit external corroboration quarterly and after any change to address, phone, ownership or supplier relationships.
