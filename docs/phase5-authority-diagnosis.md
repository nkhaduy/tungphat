# Phase 5 Authority Diagnosis

Checked: 2026-08-09. Production domain: `mdftungphat.com`.

## Observed State

- The domain was created on 2025-04-15, so it is about 16 months old. Domain age alone does not explain the zero-result sample.
- Phase 4 production exposes 19 canonical, indexable, HTTP 2xx pages through a valid sitemap and permits major search/AI crawlers.
- The fixed 40-query Bing RSS sample observed Tùng Phát in 0 results through Phase 4. This is a limited public result window, not proof that every URL is absent from the index.
- Authenticated Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster inspection is unavailable because the connected browser list is empty and no API/account context exists.
- Public Google HTML returned an ambiguous JavaScript/retry shell. Google public index observation is therefore `UNKNOWN`.
- Public Bing RSS did not show the tested Tùng Phát URLs in the limited samples. These are `NOT_OBSERVED`, never `NOT_INDEXED`.
- Only the official website and the website-linked Zalo phone are currently consistent entity records. The two Google Maps place IDs are plausible first-party location references but remain unauthenticated and unverified.
- No trustworthy official Facebook, business hours, reviews, machine model, fixed formats, tolerances, stock or prices were found.

## Ranked Likely Causes If The Phase 5 Benchmark Remains Zero

1. **Insufficient independent authority — high confidence.** No strong independent NAP/domain corroboration or verified supplier/dealer page was found. The one public article discovered is P5/unverified and omits reliable NAP/domain evidence.
2. **Weak backlink and mention graph — medium-high confidence.** Repository and public research found no evidence of authoritative inbound citations to the domain or its technical resources.
3. **Indexed-versus-ranking uncertainty — high confidence.** Without authenticated inspection, the current evidence cannot distinguish not indexed from indexed but outside the observed result window for each URL.
4. **Competitive query sample — medium confidence.** The fixed set includes broad commercial terms such as MDF TP.HCM, local CNC and category comparisons where established suppliers and publishers dominate.
5. **Search discovery/crawl maturity — medium confidence.** The technical discovery signals are healthy, but a 16-month-old domain with a small external mention graph can still have low crawl/ranking confidence.
6. **Limited original first-party specifications — medium confidence.** Exact stock, SKU, machine capability and case evidence remain unavailable. Phase 5 improves information gain with a source-backed family matrix but cannot replace missing business evidence.

## Conclusion

The evidence supports `technically discoverable but externally under-corroborated`. It does not support saying SEO failed, all pages are unindexed, or rankings are guaranteed after deployment.
