# Tùng Phát Writing

## Mission

Write Vietnamese website content for **Tùng Phát – ván gỗ công nghiệp & gia công CNC** so it sounds like it came from a knowledgeable material supplier and workshop team, not from an SEO content generator.

The target voice is:

> **Người bán ván hiểu vật liệu và xưởng, nói rõ cho khách chọn đúng – gọn, thực tế, có kinh nghiệm, không tự tâng bốc.**

The reader should feel that Tùng Phát understands what they are buying, what can go wrong in fabrication, what information is needed for a quote, and which option is practical for the job.

## When to use this skill

Use this skill whenever a task creates or changes public-facing Vietnamese copy for Tùng Phát, including:

- homepage and landing pages;
- product/category pages;
- MDF, MFC, Plywood, gỗ ghép and surface-material pages;
- CNC/cutting/drilling/grooving/service pages;
- local SEO pages for Thủ Đức / TP.HCM;
- knowledge articles and FAQs;
- CTA, cards, captions, labels, meta title and meta description;
- rewrites intended to make existing content more natural or less AI-like.

For technical SEO implementation, schema, redirects, crawl/indexing, metadata architecture, or performance work, combine this skill with the repository's SEO skill/rules. This skill owns **voice and content quality**, not every technical SEO decision.

## 1. Brand voice

### Core characteristics

Write with these traits, in this order:

1. **Concrete** — name the material, thickness, surface, machining step, file, dimension, use case or location instead of speaking abstractly.
2. **Useful** — answer the buyer's next question, not the writer's need to fill a section.
3. **Natural** — sentences should sound plausible when spoken by a knowledgeable salesperson or workshop operator.
4. **Technically careful** — distinguish moisture resistance from waterproofing; distinguish core board from decorative surface; do not overstate performance.
5. **Commercial without hype** — help the customer buy, compare, send specs or request a quote without claiming Tùng Phát is “the best”.
6. **Local when relevant** — Thủ Đức, Tam Bình and TP.HCM are useful context, not keywords to repeat mechanically.

### Default relationship with the reader

Prefer **implicit second person** or **“bạn”** when direct address improves clarity.

Good:

- “Nếu làm tủ bếp, nên tách rõ phần nào gần nước trước khi chọn cốt ván.”
- “Bạn đã có file CNC thì gửi kèm vật liệu, độ dày và số lượng để kiểm tra nhanh hơn.”
- “Với hậu tủ, thân tủ và cánh tủ, độ dày thường không giống nhau.”

Avoid switching between “bạn”, “quý khách”, “khách hàng”, “người tiêu dùng” in the same page unless the surface requires formal language.

## 2. What to learn from competitors — and what NOT to copy

Competitors are references for **communication patterns only**, never a source of Tùng Phát facts.

### KES Group — keep

- answer the section question early;
- explain technical distinctions clearly;
- use tables only when comparison genuinely becomes easier;
- connect material properties to real applications.

### KES Group — avoid

- repeated formula: “X gồm … Sau đây … giúp bạn …”;
- starting every H2 with a dictionary-style definition;
- turning every paragraph into a perfectly symmetrical SEO block;
- repeating the brand/product series in every section.

### Hoàng Sơn Lâm — keep

- direct commercial language;
- product-first hierarchy;
- clear local/service-area context;
- easy movement from product interest to contact.

### Hoàng Sơn Lâm — avoid

- keyword-stuffed titles or location chains;
- unsupported “giá cạnh tranh”, “tiêu chuẩn khắt khe”, etc.;
- copying category wording just because it ranks.

### Vật Liệu Lộc Phát — keep

- conversational transitions;
- practical explanations for non-specialists;
- straightforward product → use case → price/contact flow.

### Vật Liệu Lộc Phát — avoid

- generic openers such as “Ngày nay…”, “Đặc biệt là…”;
- absolute claims like lifespan, health safety, termite/water resistance without evidence;
- filler conclusions and sales clichés.

### Hoàng Gia — keep

- short benefit blocks;
- scannable product/use-case sections;
- simple wording that does not require specialist knowledge.

### Hoàng Gia — avoid

- “đơn vị hàng đầu”, “hoàn hảo”, “tối ưu”, “bền đẹp”, “đồng hành cùng bạn” style claims;
- decorative adjectives that do not help a purchasing decision.

Never imitate a competitor's distinctive wording sentence-for-sentence.

## 3. Tùng Phát's anti-AI rules

### Hard-ban common AI filler

Delete or rewrite these unless the phrase is genuinely necessary in context:

- “Trong bối cảnh…”
- “Ngày nay…”
- “Hiện nay…” when it adds no time meaning
- “Không chỉ … mà còn …”
- “Mang đến giải pháp…”
- “Giải pháp tối ưu”
- “Đáp ứng đa dạng nhu cầu”
- “Nâng tầm không gian”
- “Vẻ đẹp hoàn mỹ”
- “Sự lựa chọn hoàn hảo”
- “Đồng hành cùng bạn”
- “Đừng ngần ngại…”
- “Hãy cùng Tùng Phát tìm hiểu…”
- “Trong bài viết này, chúng ta sẽ…”
- “Dưới đây là … giúp bạn …” when the heading already says it
- “Với nhiều năm kinh nghiệm…” unless a verified number/source exists
- “uy tín”, “hàng đầu”, “chất lượng cao”, “giá tốt”, “giá cạnh tranh” without specific evidence

### Avoid bureaucratic/robotic hedging

Do not repeat words like:

- “đối chiếu”;
- “xác nhận”;
- “đã xác định”;
- “đang được giới thiệu”;
- “theo nhu cầu thực tế”;
- “cần kiểm tra”;
- “theo dữ liệu hiện có”.

These are allowed only where uncertainty truly matters: **stock, current price, exact supplier SKU, lot-dependent dimensions/finish, machining feasibility, lead time**.

If several uncertainties apply to one page, consolidate them into **one natural note near the quote/contact CTA** instead of adding a caveat to every section.

Example:

Bad:
> “Quy cách tấm cần kiểm tra theo nhà cung cấp. Độ dày cần xác nhận theo lô hàng. Mã màu cần đối chiếu khi báo giá.”

Better:
> “Quy cách, mã màu và tồn kho có thể thay đổi theo từng dòng hàng. Gửi mã hoặc kích thước bạn cần, Tùng Phát sẽ kiểm tra trước khi báo giá.”

### Avoid AI-perfect rhythm

Do not make every section look like:

1. one-sentence definition;
2. one generic paragraph;
3. exactly four bullets;
4. one transition sentence.

Vary the rhythm. Some sections can be two concise paragraphs. Some need bullets. Some need a small table. Some need only one direct answer.

### Sentence rhythm

Mix sentence lengths naturally:

- short sentence for the main point;
- medium sentence for explanation;
- longer sentence only when comparing conditions or technical trade-offs.

Prefer active verbs: **cắt, phủ, ép, khoan, soi, bắt vít, làm hậu tủ, làm cánh, gửi file, kiểm tra mã, báo giá**.

Use fewer nominal phrases like “việc thực hiện gia công”, “khả năng cung ứng vật liệu”, “nội dung yêu cầu”.

## 4. Fact safety — never hallucinate product claims

### Source priority

For Tùng Phát-specific facts, use this order:

1. repository data/content and current CMS/product records;
2. official manufacturer/supplier catalogue or technical datasheet;
3. current verified Tùng Phát production page if canonical data is not elsewhere;
4. reputable general technical sources for generic material knowledge.

Competitor websites are **never** authoritative sources for Tùng Phát's price, stock, standards, brands, dimensions, branches, machines, history or service capability.

### Never invent

Do not invent or infer:

- current stock;
- price or discount;
- delivery time;
- exact board size/thickness available at Tùng Phát;
- emission standard;
- moisture/swelling percentage;
- origin/country;
- “official distributor” status;
- years of experience;
- project counts;
- machine tolerances/capacity;
- warranty;
- health or environmental claims;
- “waterproof”, “termite-proof”, “fireproof” performance;
- supplier relationship.

If an exact fact is not verified, either omit it or write the useful stable fact around it. Do not fill the page with disclaimers just to compensate for missing data.

### Technical precision examples

Prefer:

- “MDF chống ẩm chịu môi trường ẩm tốt hơn MDF thường; không nên hiểu là ván chống nước hoàn toàn.”
- “Melamine là lớp bề mặt trang trí; khả năng chịu ẩm còn phụ thuộc cốt ván, cạnh và điều kiện sử dụng.”

Avoid:

- “MDF lõi xanh chống nước.”
- “MDF không bị mối mọt.”
- “Ván an toàn tuyệt đối cho sức khỏe.”

## 5. SEO without sounding like SEO

### Search intent first

Before drafting, identify the page's primary intent:

- learn/compare;
- find a supplier locally;
- browse products/mã màu;
- check price;
- request CNC/fabrication;
- prepare a file/spec for quoting.

Write for that intent first. Keywords should follow naturally.

### Heading rules

- H1: one clear topic + useful modifier/location only when needed.
- H2: answer real buyer questions.
- Avoid putting “Thủ Đức”, “TP.HCM”, “giá rẻ”, “uy tín” into every heading.
- Do not create a heading just because an SEO outline expects another keyword variation.

Good H2 examples:

- “MDF thường và MDF chống ẩm khác nhau ở đâu?”
- “Làm tủ bếp nên chọn cốt nào?”
- “Gửi gì cho xưởng để báo giá CNC?”
- “Khi nào nên chọn Plywood thay vì MDF?”

### Keyword use

Use the primary keyword in the H1, early body copy and where semantically natural. Then use real synonyms and adjacent terms.

Never repeat exact-match keywords simply to reach a density target.

### Local SEO

Local signals should sound like logistics:

- where the branches are;
- who the service is convenient for;
- what customers can do before visiting;
- what can be sent via Zalo;
- which local area the service realistically covers.

Do not write paragraphs that are only lists of districts/provinces.

### Internal links

Anchor text should describe the destination naturally:

- “xem bảng mã Melamine”
- “tham khảo cách chuẩn bị file CNC”
- “xem nhóm MDF chống ẩm”

Avoid repeating the same keyword-rich anchor on one page.

### FAQ

Only include questions a buyer could plausibly ask. Answer directly in 1–3 short paragraphs. Do not generate FAQ solely to occupy SERP space.

## 6. Page patterns

### Homepage

Goal: immediately establish **what Tùng Phát sells + what Tùng Phát can machine + where**.

Preferred order:

1. clear value proposition;
2. main material groups;
3. CNC/service capability;
4. practical reason to choose/contact;
5. key buyer information for quoting;
6. local branches;
7. useful knowledge/content.

Do not make the homepage read like a compliance notice. Stable facts should be stated confidently; uncertainty belongs near price/stock/quote actions.

### Product/category page

Preferred flow:

1. what the material is in plain language;
2. when it is a good fit;
3. important variants or surfaces;
4. strengths and limitations;
5. practical applications;
6. how to choose;
7. current product/mã links if available;
8. quote CTA.

Do not pad with a long manufacturing history unless search intent genuinely asks for it.

### CNC/service page

Write from the job flow, not from marketing adjectives:

1. what Tùng Phát can do;
2. compatible inputs/material context;
3. what file/spec the customer should send;
4. checks before machining;
5. common job types;
6. quote/contact CTA.

Use workshop vocabulary accurately and plainly.

### Knowledge article

Answer the query in the first 1–2 paragraphs. Then explain only what helps the reader make a decision.

A useful article often includes:

- plain definition;
- key distinction;
- where it works / where it does not;
- comparison table if useful;
- buying/fabrication advice;
- Tùng Phát CTA that matches the article intent.

Do not force every article into the same outline.

### Meta title / description

Meta title:

- readable first;
- typically one main intent;
- brand at the end when useful;
- no keyword chains.

Meta description:

- explain what the visitor will get;
- include one concrete differentiator or action;
- avoid empty phrases like “uy tín, chất lượng, giá tốt”.

## 8. Before / after examples

### Example A — current over-defensive tone

Avoid:

> “Các trang chi tiết và hình ảnh dưới đây được nối tới nội dung đã có trong website; mã hàng và tình trạng cung cấp được xác nhận khi trao đổi thực tế.”

Prefer:

> “Xem nhanh các nhóm ván Tùng Phát đang giới thiệu. Nếu đã có mã, độ dày hoặc bề mặt cần tìm, gửi qua Zalo để kiểm tra hàng và báo giá.”

### Example B — repeated verification language

Avoid:

> “Vật liệu, độ dày, đơn vị đo và đường gia công cần được chốt trước khi sản xuất.”

Prefer:

> “Trước khi chạy máy, cần chốt vật liệu, độ dày, đơn vị đo và đúng đường cắt trong file.”

### Example C — generic AI marketing

Avoid:

> “Tùng Phát mang đến giải pháp ván gỗ toàn diện, đáp ứng đa dạng nhu cầu và nâng tầm không gian sống.”

Prefer:

> “Tùng Phát cung cấp MDF, MFC, Plywood, gỗ ghép và nhận cắt/CNC theo quy cách. Khách có thể chọn vật liệu rồi gửi kích thước hoặc file để làm tiếp tại cùng một đầu mối.”

### Example D — natural material advice

Prefer:

> “MDF thường hợp với các hạng mục khô ráo như tủ áo, bàn hoặc vách trong nhà. Với khu vực dễ ẩm, nên xem dòng MDF chống ẩm và xử lý cạnh cẩn thận; lõi xanh không có nghĩa là tấm ván có thể ngâm nước.”

## 9. Mandatory human edit pass

After the first draft, perform a second pass called **HUMAN PASS**.

For each section:

1. Remove any sentence that only repeats the heading.
2. Replace abstract nouns with concrete material/workshop words.
3. Remove repeated “Tùng Phát” where the subject is already obvious.
4. Remove repeated caveats; keep the uncertainty note only where the purchase decision needs it.
5. Break any paragraph that feels generated from a fixed template.
6. Check that at least some sentences are short and direct.
7. Remove unsupported superlatives and promises.
8. Make CTA wording specific to the user's next action.
9. Read it mentally as spoken Vietnamese: if a salesperson would never say the sentence, rewrite it.
10. Confirm the page still satisfies search intent after removing filler.

### Anti-AI smell test

A page fails if 3 or more of these are true:

- three adjacent sections start with the same grammar pattern;
- “giúp bạn”, “phù hợp”, “đáp ứng”, “đảm bảo” repeat excessively;
- every section has exactly the same number of bullets;
- brand name appears unnaturally often;
- many sentences could apply to any building-material company;
- copy sounds cautious everywhere instead of only around variable facts;
- there is more explanation of the page than explanation of the material/job;
- adjectives outnumber useful dimensions, applications, actions or trade-offs.

Rewrite until the smell test passes.

## 10. Output contract for Codex

When asked to create or rewrite content:

1. Inspect the existing page/data before writing.
2. Identify the primary search/buyer intent.
3. Verify Tùng Phát-specific facts from repository/CMS/source data.
4. Draft with this skill.
5. Run HUMAN PASS.
6. Keep the existing design/content schema unless the task explicitly changes it.
7. Run the relevant content/SEO/build checks required by the repository.
8. For production tasks, follow `AGENTS.md`: run the required verification before deployment, deploy, then verify the live production site rather than claiming completion from localhost.

When reporting the result, explicitly mention any content claim you intentionally removed because it was unsupported.
